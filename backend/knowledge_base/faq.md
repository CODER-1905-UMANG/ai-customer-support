# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is TechNova?

TechNova is an online technology store that sells electronics,
computer accessories, smart devices, and other technology products.

## How can I check my order?

Customers can check their order status by providing their order number.
The support system can retrieve the latest order status from the order
system.

## How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 7 business days after the order
has been confirmed.

Delivery time may vary depending on the customer's location, product
availability, and courier operations.

## Can I cancel my order?

An order can be cancelled before it enters the shipping process.
Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may no longer be available.

## Can I request a refund?

Customers can request a refund when the order meets the conditions
specified in the refund policy.

Refund eligibility depends on the product and reason for the request.

## What payment methods are supported?

TechNova supports:

- Credit cards
- Debit cards
- UPI
- Net banking
- Digital wallets

## What should I do if my payment was deducted but my order failed?

Customers should provide their order number so the support system can
check both the order and payment status.

If the payment was successful but the order failed, the issue may require
further investigation or escalation to human support.

## How can I contact human support?

Customers can explicitly request human support.

The AI support system may also escalate an issue when it cannot resolve
the customer's problem or when manual investigation is required.

## What information should I provide when contacting support?

Customers should provide relevant information such as:

- Order number
- Payment information
- Description of the issue
- Relevant product information

Providing accurate information helps the support team investigate the
issue more efficiently.
