# Shipping Policy

## Standard Delivery

Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 7 business days after an order
has been confirmed.

Delivery time may vary depending on:

- Customer location
- Product availability
- Courier operations
- Public holidays
- Unexpected delivery delays

## Order Tracking

Customers can request the current status of their order by providing
their order number.

The support system can retrieve the latest order status from the order
system.

## Order Status

An order may have different statuses during its lifecycle, including:

- Pending
- Confirmed
- Processing
- Shipped
- Out for delivery
- Delivered
- Cancelled
- Failed

## Delivery Delays

If an order is delayed beyond the expected delivery period, the
customer should contact support with the order number.

The support system may check the order status and provide the latest
available information.

## Address Changes

Customers should contact support as soon as possible if they need to
correct their delivery address.

Address changes may not be possible after the order has entered the
shipping process.

## Failed Delivery

If a delivery attempt fails, the customer should contact support with
the order number.

Further action may depend on the courier status and the reason for the
failed delivery.
