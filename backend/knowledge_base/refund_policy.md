# Refund Policy

## Refund Eligibility

Customers may request a refund for eligible products when the request
meets the applicable refund conditions.

Refund eligibility may depend on:

- Product condition
- Reason for the refund
- Time since delivery
- Product category
- Whether the product is eligible for return

## Refund Request

Customers should provide their order number when requesting a refund.

The support system should verify the order information before creating
a refund-related support request.

## Refund for Failed Orders

If an order fails but the payment was successfully completed, the
payment status should be verified before determining the next action.

If the issue cannot be resolved automatically, the case may be
escalated to human support.

## Refund Processing

Once a refund is approved, the refund is processed through the original
payment method where applicable.

The time required for the refund to appear may depend on the payment
provider or bank.

## Non-Eligible Refunds

A refund may not be available when the product or request does not meet
the applicable refund conditions.

Customers should contact support if they believe a refund should be
available but the system indicates that they are not eligible.

## Support

If a customer is unable to resolve a refund issue through the available
support process, they can request human assistance.

The support system may create a ticket and escalate the issue for
manual investigation.
