# Account Policy

## Account Information

Customers should provide accurate account information when using
TechNova services.

## Account Access Issues

Customers who cannot access their account should contact support and
provide information necessary to verify the account.

The support system should not request sensitive credentials such as
passwords or one-time passwords.

## Account Information Changes

Customers may request updates to eligible account information through
the available account-management process.

Some changes may require additional verification.

## Security

Customers should never share:

- Passwords
- One-time passwords
- Payment PINs
- CVVs
- Authentication codes

Support representatives will not request sensitive authentication
credentials.

## Human Support

Account issues requiring manual verification or investigation may be
escalated to human support.
