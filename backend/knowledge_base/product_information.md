# Product Information

The Wireless Headphones are designed for everyday music, calls, and
general audio use.

## Mechanical Keyboard

The Mechanical Keyboard is designed for typing, programming, and
general computer use.

Product category: Computer Accessories

## Smart Watch

The Smart Watch provides common smartwatch features for everyday use.

Product category: Wearable Technology

The Bluetooth Speaker is a portable audio device designed for wireless
music playback.

Product category: Audio
